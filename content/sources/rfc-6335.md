## Summary

Best Current Practice 165 (2011): how IANA hands out port
numbers and service names. Only section 6, the three port ranges, is
used here.

## Key claims

- Ports are 16-bit numbers, shared as a scheme across TCP, UDP, SCTP and DCCP. "TCP, UDP, UDP-Lite, SCTP, and DCCP use 16-bit namespaces for their port number registries." (6)
- System (well-known) ports are 0-1023. "the System Ports, also known as the Well Known Ports, from 0-1023 (assigned by IANA)" (6)
- User (registered) ports are 1024-49151. "the User Ports, also known as the Registered Ports" (6, range 1024 to 49151, assigned by IANA)
- Dynamic (ephemeral) ports are 49152-65535 and never assigned. "the Dynamic Ports, also known as the Private or Ephemeral Ports, from 49152-65535 (never assigned)" (6)
