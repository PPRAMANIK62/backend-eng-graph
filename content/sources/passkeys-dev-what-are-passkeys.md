## Summary

Short intro page: passkeys are unique per service, the server stores
only public keys, and the browser and OS, not the user, decide which
site a passkey is used on.

## Key claims

- Unique per service. "By design, passkeys are unique per-service." (Automatically unique)
- Servers store public keys; sync services can't use the private keys. "Relying Party (RP) servers store public keys." and "Even servers that assist in the syncing of passkeys across a user’s devices never have the ability to view or use the private keys for a user’s passkeys." (Breach-resistant)
- Phishing resistance comes from the software, not the person. "browsers and operating systems enforce that passkeys are only ever used for the appropriate service." (Phishing-resistant)
