## Summary

An Experimental RFC from Google engineers raising TCP's initial
congestion window from 2 to 4 segments to about 10.

## Key claims

- The initial window goes to 10 segments, capped at 14600 bytes. "window (IW) to 10 segments (maximum 14600 B)" (1)
- The formula. "min (10*MSS, max (2*MSS, 14600))" (2, equation 1)
- Status is Experimental. (header, "Category: Experimental")
