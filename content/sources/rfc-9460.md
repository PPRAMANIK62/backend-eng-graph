## Summary

Defines two newer record types, SVCB and HTTPS (November 2023). They
tell a client, before it connects, which endpoints serve a name and with
what settings (protocols, ports, keys), and they allow aliasing at the
zone apex, which CNAME can't do.

## Key claims

- What they're for. "SVCB records allow a service to be provided from multiple alternative endpoints, each with associated parameters (such as transport protocol configuration)" (Abstract)
- They allow apex aliasing, unlike CNAME. "They also enable aliasing of apex domains, which is not possible with CNAME." (Abstract)
- HTTPS is SVCB specialized for HTTP. "The HTTPS RR is a variation of SVCB for use with HTTP" (Abstract)
- CNAME can't carry configuration. "CNAME is also inadequate when the service operator needs to provide a bound collection of consistent configuration parameters through the DNS" (1)
- Goal: CNAME-like behavior at the apex. "Enables CNAME-like functionality at a zone apex (such as "example.com")" (1.1)
